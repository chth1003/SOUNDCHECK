Dresscode: Stormy

There are no strict rules, but the evening's atmosphere is dark, blue and slightly eerie.

  • Colors: midnight blue, black, silver, purple and white details.
  • Things that catch the light: glitter, metal, lacquer or light fabrics.
  • Feathers, wings or a tiara are very welcome.
  • Choose clothes and shoes you can dance in.
